                              Addendum to Structure Scope of Work

Following strengthening measures are to be carried out before installation of new bridges:

 Sl. No.   Platform                            Strengthening Required
             Name
 1         ICG      One member (660 X 12.7 mm) at Jacket walkway level (El +6.96 m) along Row
                    B between Rows 2 and 3 (15m Length) shall be strengthened with 10 mm thick
                    wrap plate (4 Quarters) through the entire length of 15m.

 2         ICP        One member (762 X 16 mm) at Jacket walkway level (El +7.8 m) along Row 1
                      between Rows A and B (13.5m Length) shall be strengthened with 12 mm thick
                      wrap plate (4 Quarters) through the entire length of 13.5m.

 3         BHS        One plan brace member (457 X 12.7 mm) at Jacket walkway level (El +6.272 m)
                      supporting the fire water caissons near Leg B1 (3 m Length portion near to fire
                      water caisson supports only) shall be strengthened with 10mm thick wrap
                      plate (4 Quarters).
