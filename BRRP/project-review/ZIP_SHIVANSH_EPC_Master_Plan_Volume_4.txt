Management procedures and control requirements
4.1 Marine strategy
Four management campaigns: strengthening, ICW–ICG LD, ICG–ICP LD and BHS–SLQ replacement/removal. These are not necessarily four charters; test consolidated versus separate campaigns for safety, workability, cost and critical-path effect.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.2 Module register
Record module ID, bridge, drawing revision, actual dimensions, measured weight, calculated and verified COG, lifting points, transport supports, preservation, acceptance dossier, vessel and installation IWP.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.3 Vessel selection
Request crane charts at actual radius/configuration, deck strength, stability, positioning, environmental limits, vessel certification, marine personnel competence, charter conditions, mobilisation and contingency arrangements.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.4 Marine engineering and assurance
Project-specific loadout, rigging, sea-fastening, voyage, deck loading, motion, lift dynamics, landing and removal analyses require competent specialist checking. Evaluate applicability of DNV-ST-N001 and contract-specific MWS obligations.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.5 Fabrication-to-offshore chain
IFC drawing → inspected fabrication → verified weight/COG → module release → shore base → approved loadout → marine transport → work-front readiness → authorised lift → inspected installation → closeout.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.6 Logistics controls
14-day cargo readiness and 72-hour vessel manifest; segregate accepted, quarantined, backload and preservation-sensitive cargo. Use cargo manifests with unique ID, destination, mass and documentation.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.7 Marine daily report
Record vessel hours by productive, transit, weather standby, operations standby, equipment failure and contractor-caused standby. Review forecast days and cost each day.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.8 BHS–SLQ special control
Coordinate new replacement structure, operating service interfaces, temporary support, approved lifting and landing, structural integrity, engineered demolition and disposal. Never infer a single-lift weight from 282 MT bridge quantity.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.9 Weather and SIMOPS
Use project-specific environmental thresholds, vessel procedures, ONGC work authorisation and SIMOPS interface documents. Vessel master and installation authorities retain safety powers.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
4.10 Marine gate checklist
Before any operation verify competent engineering approval, certified equipment, inspection releases, permits, isolations, communications, weather limits, emergency readiness, vessel configuration and formal go/no-go.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.

Formal review and release criteria
