                              Important Notes
1    Vendors with no remark in this list are confirmed vendors.

     The 'On Hold' status for vendors has been categorised into following groups:

                        On Hold due to Non-submission of Name change / Other
              B1
                              documents by the vendor to ONGC

              B2         On Hold due to No response from the vendor

2                       On Hold due to Poor / Negative Performance Feedback
              B3
                                  received against the vendor from ONGC Asset.

          Banned Firms      Banned Firm under Corporate MM Portal

     The reasons for which a vendor has been put 'On Hold' have been marked against the
      vendor as B1, B2 & B3.
3      Vendors, who are traders, have been identified

     The contractor can continue to get quotation from vendors who are 'On Hold' under group
       'B1', provided that the reasons for which a vendor has been put 'on hold' are resolved by
      the vendor at the time of submission of Purchase Specification (PS)

     Vendors in Group 'B2' will be delisted automatically in case No-response is received from
      Vendor.However,the LSTK contractors are free to contact these vendors, if they respond to
      them.4
     The 'On Hold' status of a vendor under group 'B3' in this Suggested Vendor List means that
      the contractor shall not place order(s) on the vendor for the product for which the vendor
     has been put 'On Hold' by ONGC.

     The 'Banned Firms' status of a vendor in this Suggested Vendor List means that the
       contractor shall not place order(s) on the vendor for the product for which the vendor has
     been put 'Banned Firms' by ONGC.

       a) Manufacturing locations of MNC vendors other than the enlisted location in the
     Suggested Vendor List, can also be accepted for supply of product after obtaining an
      Undertaking in the prescribed proforma from Principal/ enlisted vendor. Such manufacturing
       locations shall possess all accreditations required for the product and comply with the
      Functional specification.The OEM vendor who is enlisted already in ONGCs SVL and wants
5     his other Manufacturing Locations / API certified fabrication yards etc., also to be accepted
      by ONGC, shall intimate all such locations where the material will be sourced to ONGC.In
        this case, the SVL shall be updated to incorporate all such locations wherefrom the items
      are being sourced. Any changes from these locations shall be intimated by the already
       enlisted OEM vendor.


      b) For any vendor, where the Purchase Orders are to be placed on agencies
      authorized by the enlisted vendor, the enlisted vendor shall furnish Authorization
        letter to the agency for a particular product with an undertaking for agreeing to
      the following conditions:-
       1) Purchase specification (For PS items) shall be submitted & signed by
       enlisted vendor only.
       2) Warrantee/Guarantee shall be provided by the enlisted vendor
       3) Enlisted Vendor shall ensure maintenance support for the product

       c) Manufacturing locations established in India by an Indian Company can also be
      considered for supply/enlistment provided such Indian Company forms a Joint venture
       registered in India with:
         1. an enlisted foreign vendor in the suggested vendor list.
         2. any foreign vendor who is not enlisted but meets the qualification criteria for  vendor
       enlistment.
     Above arrangement shall be considered subject to meeting the following requirements:
             (i) Undertaking in the prescribed proforma from both the partners shall be submitted.
              (ii) The manufacturing location established in India shall possess all the accreditations
      required for the product and also comply with the functional specifications.
              (iii) Factory inspection may be carried out to ascertain the capability of the Joint Venture
     Company.
           (iv) In the event of collapse of such joint venture arrangement, supply from the JV
     company shall not be accepted.

      d) Manufacturing locations established in India by an Indian Company can also be
      considered for supply/ enlistment for particular product with an undertaking in the prescribed
      Proforma from the enlisted foreign vendor, provided such Indian company has technical
      License agreement with an enlisted foreign vendor in the suggested vendor list.
     Above arrangement shall be considered subject to meeting the following requirements:-
          I. The Technical License agreement shall be valid for minimum 3 years on the date of
      submission of PQD. After expiry of Technical License agreement , renewed agreement
       shall be required to be submitted.
          II. In case of cancellation/non renewal of Technical License agreement, the name of the
      vendor shall be delisted.
          III. Undertaking in the prescribed proforma from enlisted foreign Vendor.
       IV. Undertaking in the prescribed proforma from Indian company.
      V. The manufacturing location established in India shall possess all the accreditations
      required for the product and also comply with the functional specifications.
       VI. Factory Inspection may be carried out to ascertain the capability of the Company.




      For enlistment of a Packager as vendor following conditions must be satisfied:
       a) Packager is the Process Licensor OR the OEM of the main equipment(Main equipment is
       listed in the respective Functional specification/ Design Criteria.
       b) If the packager is not Process Licensor or OEM of main equipment, the packager shall
      submit the authorization letter from the ONGC enlisted Vendor of main equipment for
       carrying out packaging activity.
       c) The Packager shall select the major items from the ONGC Suggested Vendor List as
6    mentioned in the respective Functional Specification/Design Criteria.
       d) Any registered Packager who is the Process Licensor OR the OEM of the main
      equipment wants to relinquish his Over-all single-point responsibility of the Packaged Item
     and transfer the same to Other Vendor(s) of the Packaged Items, he may do so provided all
      such major sub-vendors of such packaged Items mutually agree to Provide the similar
      Services to ONGC under the leadership of the new sub-vendor who accepts to take single
      Point responsibility.

     Vendor should agree / guarantee supply of spares for the entire life cycle of the item /
7    equipment (i.e. 7 years in case of electronic equipment/items and 10 years in case of
      mechanical equipment/items)

8    Vendor should agree to comply with ONGC Terms & Conditions for Supply of Spares
