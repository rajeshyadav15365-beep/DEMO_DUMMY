Management procedures and control requirements
2.1 Design basis and document hierarchy
Use the signed contract, latest addenda/corrigenda, ONGC SOW, engineering specifications, existing bridge reports, survey results and approved drawings in that order as contractually defined. Establish a master document register with revision and supersession control.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.2 Bridge-by-bridge engineering deliverables
For each bridge: survey and condition assessment, member schedule, structural design checks, fatigue assessment as applicable, landing/deck connections, temporary works, lifting interfaces, fabrication drawings, weld maps, coating schedule and as-builts. No design is approved by this document.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.3 MTO measurement rules
Maintain line-item dimensions, material grades, unit weights, fabrication allowances, procurement quantity, installed quantity, removed quantity and salvage/backload. Link each row to drawing number and revision. Avoid treating bridge total as individual lift weight.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.4 Quantity discrepancy
Source SOW table totals structural columns as 367+361+197+217 = 1,142 MT; sum of printed bridge-level structural entries = 1,141 MT. Keep an open technical query for 1 MT until the controlling MTO and drawings are reconciled.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.5 Procurement work packages
Steel and sections; bolts and fasteners; grating; handrails; GI sheeting; coatings; welding consumables; lifting attachments; temporary works; inspection/NDT; E&I and service interfaces if required. Use approved specifications and inspection categories.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.6 Vendor enquiry and technical bid evaluation
RFQ includes quantities, specifications, drawing revisions, inspection/test plan, traceability, delivery dates, documentation, deviations and warranty. Pass/fail technical compliance precedes normalised commercial comparison.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.7 Long-lead and expediting
Maintain enquiry date, bid due, TBE, PO, drawing approval, raw-material release, production, inspection, dispatch and work-front required-on-site dates. Escalate any negative float to Project Director.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.8 Material control
Unique heat/batch traceability; receiving inspection; quarantine of nonconforming material; controlled storage and preservation; issue by bridge IWP; reconcile excess and shortage weekly.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.9 Engineering release criteria
Only checked and approved IFC drawings may release fabrication or offshore work. Field changes require TQ/RFI, engineering disposition, revision control and affected-work reassessment.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
2.10 Procurement KPI
PO coverage, material availability by IWP, supplier on-time delivery, expediting exceptions, inspected/accepted quantity, outstanding TQs and forecast cost of material.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.

Formal review and release criteria
Before release, discipline leads must verify: (1) controlling contract and drawing revision; (2) reconciled quantity and scope; (3) qualified resources and documented capacity; (4) schedule and cost impact; (5) inspection and safety hold points; (6) ONGC and marine operational authorisations where applicable. CEO approval of a planning volume does not authorise offshore operations.
