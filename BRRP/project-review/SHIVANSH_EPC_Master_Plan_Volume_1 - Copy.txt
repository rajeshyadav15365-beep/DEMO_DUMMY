Management procedures and control requirements
1.1 Executive mandate
Shivansh shall operate as a single accountable EPC integrator, controlling engineering, procurement, fabrication, marine and offshore work through one project director, one WBS, one controlled schedule and one cost ledger. This is a pre-award planning issue, not evidence of award or approved execution.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.2 Contract strategy and authority
Create a clause-by-clause obligations matrix: commencement, 360-day completion, scope inclusions, free-issue material, guarantees, insurance, liquidated damages, invoicing, subcontracting permission, variation and notification time bars. Contract terms override illustrative plans.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.3 Governance and organisation
CEO / steering committee; Project Director; Engineering, Procurement, Fabrication, Marine, Offshore Construction, QA/QC, HSE, Project Controls, Contracts and Finance Managers. Issue delegation-of-authority and RACI matrices. Maintain separate ONGC operations and vessel-master authority.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.4 Stage gates
G0 bid/award; G1 baseline reconciliation; G2 engineering release; G3 purchase authorisation; G4 fabrication readiness; G5 module release; G6 marine readiness; G7 offshore work permit and SIMOPS; G8 inspection acceptance; G9 handover. Each gate requires dated signed evidence.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.5 15-day pre-award mobilisation
Days 1–3 contract/MTO/drawing registers; 2–7 engineering, fabrication and marine market enquiries; 5–10 labour and vessel feasibility; 8–12 schedule and finance; 13–15 independent CEO review. Use conditional, nonbinding reservations before award.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.6 Management operating rhythm
Daily shift and marine coordination; 72-hour executable look-ahead; weekly six-week constraint register and resource forecast; monthly integrated forecast-at-completion; CEO decisions by exception.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.7 Performance dashboard
Track accepted quantity, earned/actual man-hours, committed resources, critical-path movement, vessel standby, NCR closure, cash runway, ONGC certification and unresolved safety gates.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.8 Approval matrix
Engineering Manager releases checked technical deliverables; Marine Manager arranges technical review; QA/QC controls inspection release; ONGC/platform and vessel authorities control operational permissions; CEO authorises major financial commitments.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
1.9 CEO decision memorandum
Proceed conditionally with pre-award technical qualification; no claim of 360-day feasibility until measured quantities, detailed logic, resource capacities, marine workability, cash availability and ONGC interfaces are verified.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.

Formal review and release criteria
Before release, discipline leads must verify: (1) controlling contract and drawing revision; (2) reconciled quantity and scope; (3) qualified resources and documented capacity; (4) schedule and cost impact; (5) inspection and safety hold points; (6) ONGC and marine operational authorisations where applicable.
