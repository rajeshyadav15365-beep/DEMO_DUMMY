Management procedures and control requirements
3.1 Production system
Use two proposed fabrication lines and one strengthening cell only as a capacity hypothesis; audit actual bays, crane reach, workforce, welding stations, coating throughput and loadout access before committing milestones.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.2 Fabrication work packs
Each pack specifies approved drawing, cut list, member/assembly IDs, weld details, fit-up tolerances, WPS, NDT, coating, dimensional control, hours and acceptance records. Segregate fabrication and offshore labour budgets.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.3 Yard flow
Receipt → cutting → marking → fit-up → welding → dimensional survey → NDT/repair → surface preparation → coating → final inspection → preservation → weigh/COG verification → release → loadout. Apply documented hold points.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.4 Bridge construction crews
Proposed Team A: ICW–ICG UD, ICG–ICP UD, SLQ–WIS. Team B: NQO–NQD, WIN–NC, SCA–SC1, SCA–SCF. Team C: ICW–ICG LD, ICG–ICP LD and BHS–SLQ, with specialist demolition/service teams. Actual sequencing requires work-front and POB verification.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.5 Installation work package
Every IWP: bounded location/scope; drawing revision; materials; permits and isolations; tools and equipment; crew competencies; marine access; risk assessment; ITP; budgeted hours; accepted quantity; completion signature.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.6 Labour budget method
For each trade, budget MH = measured activity quantity × approved MH/unit × documented condition factor. Measure weld-metres, connection counts, lifting pieces, access area and coating area; tonnes alone are insufficient.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.7 Offshore production reporting
Daily report records planned/accepted quantity, actual charged hours, earned hours, downtime code, constraints, inspections, next 72-hour forecast and crew/equipment utilisation. Use LPF = actual MH / earned MH.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.8 Demolition restrictions
BHS–SLQ demolition must have checked structural stability and load-path assessment, approved cut and lift sequence, isolation and service continuity controls, marine assurance and ONGC authorisation. No sequence is prescribed in this plan.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.9 Resource balancing
Level trades, welding/NDT, scaffolding, accommodation, marine resources and inspection against the actual calendar. Recover delays by removing constraints before adding crews.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.10 Construction quality gates
Hold at receiving inspection, fit-up as required, welding/NDT, dimensional checks, coating, lifting release, connection completion and final acceptance. Work may not pass a hold without the designated release.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
3.11 Six-week look-ahead
For each IWP mark engineering, materials, access, marine, personnel, equipment, permit and inspection as ready/not ready; assign owner and due date for each constraint.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.

Formal review and release criteria
Before release, discipline leads must verify: (1) controlling contract and drawing revision; (2) reconciled quantity and scope; (3) qualified resources and documented capacity; (4) schedule and cost impact; (5) inspection and safety hold points; (6) ONGC and marine operational authorisations where applicable. CEO approval of a planning volume does not authorise offshore operations.
