ii) Mechanical Properties

These tests shall include tensile testing, impact strength and bend test.
Bend tests shall be performed in accordance with ASTN A 370 with a
plunger dia equal to 4 times the thickness. No defect shall be visible on
bending.

iii) Hardness Tests

Each qualification test shall include a cross sectional hardness survey.
