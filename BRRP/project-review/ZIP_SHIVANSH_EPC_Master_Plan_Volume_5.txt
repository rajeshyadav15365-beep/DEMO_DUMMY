Management procedures and control requirements
5.1 Scheduling basis
Day 1 is the contractually effective commencement date after award, not the present date. All periods below are preliminary planning windows, not an approved critical-path programme.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.2 WBS and coding
Use BR01–BR10 and common packages ENG, MAT, FAB, QA, LOG, MAR, CON, DEM, COM. Each activity has unique ID, predecessor/successor, calendar, responsible owner, quantities, manpower/equipment and acceptance milestone.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.3 Provisional milestones
D1–30 contract baseline, surveys and engineering; D31–60 procurement/fabrication mobilisation; D61–110 fabrication and work-front preparation; D85–230 strengthening campaign; D110–180 two lower-deck installation campaigns; D170–240 BHS–SLQ; D241–300 residual offshore completion; D301–360 punch closure and handover. All overlap and seasonality must be validated.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.4 Resource planning
Model fabrication bays, cranes, steel deliveries, welders, riggers, scaffolders, NDT personnel, offshore POB, marine spreads, logistics slots and finance limits as constraints. No double booking.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.5 Schedule validation
First run logic-only CPM, then resource-constrained levelling, then costed recovery options and risk-adjusted completion. Retain baseline and change logs. Calculate actual longest path and near-critical paths.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.6 Manpower histograms
Derive crew counts from trade-specific remaining MH divided by available productive hours; include rotation, fatigue and access limits. Hypothetical 18-person teams are not a committed workforce.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.7 Weekly control
Weekly update actual start/finish, accepted quantities, earned/actual MH, procurement commitments, marine days and revised completion forecast. Freeze a six-week look-ahead and a 72-hour execution plan.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.8 Risk schedule simulation
Test engineering delay, fabrication underperformance, vessel unavailability, severe weather, ONGC access restriction, NDT backlog and working-capital shortage. Record mitigation cost and revised finish.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.9 Recovery hierarchy
Remove constraints, resequence approved work fronts, reallocate existing resources, add verified capacity, then consider costed acceleration. Never compress mandatory hold points.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.10 CEO schedule acceptance
Approve baseline only after contract dates, MTO, drawings, capacity audit, vessel workability, ONGC access and funding are evidenced. 360-day feasibility is not currently demonstrated.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
5.11 Reporting outputs
Level-1 CEO milestone map; Level-2 bridge integrated plan; Level-3 CPM schedule; Level-4 six-week look-ahead; Level-5 daily IWP/shift plan.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.

Formal review and release criteria
Before release, discipline leads must verify: (1) controlling contract and drawing revision; (2) reconciled quantity and scope; (3) qualified resources and documented capacity; (4) schedule and cost impact; (5) inspection and safety hold points; (6) ONGC and marine operational authorisations where applicable. CEO approval of a planning volume does not authorise offshore operations.
