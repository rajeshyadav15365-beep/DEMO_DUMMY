Management procedures and control requirements
6.1 Cost baseline
Code engineering, steel, fabrication, NDT, coating, logistics, marine, offshore labour, demolition, service interfaces, management, insurance, finance, risk and handover by bridge and common package.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.2 Commercial contract review
Verify ONGC invoice measurement, certification periods, retention, guarantees, advances, variation notices, tax treatment, insurance, payment terms and subcontracting permission before making assumptions.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.3 Working capital
Forecast cash-out from dated POs, subcontract milestones, wages, charters and overhead; cash-in from contract-specific billing and expected collection; peak cash gap is maximum cumulative payments minus receipts.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.4 Cost and earned value
Track budget, commitment, accrual, actual, earned value, forecast ETC and EAC. Reconcile physical progress, certified progress, invoicing and cash collected separately.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.5 Quality management
Issue project quality plan, ITP matrix, welding qualifications, material traceability, NDT/coating procedures, calibration, NCR and corrective action, turnover dossiers. ONGC/third-party hold points as contract requires.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.6 HSE and SIMOPS
Site-specific risk assessments, permit-to-work, isolations, working at height, lifting, dropped objects, hot work, marine transfers, emergency response, personnel competence and simultaneous operations must follow approved ONGC and vessel systems.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.7 Emergency response
Integrate platform emergency authority, vessel master, contractor personnel accountability, communications, evacuation, medical response, incident management and restart checks. Run tabletop and relevant joint drills.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.8 Risk and contingency
Maintain probability/impact register with named owner, early-warning indicator, mitigation, cost and schedule consequence; quantify contingency separately from hidden budget allowances.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.9 Claims and changes
Maintain change register, written instructions, contemporaneous records, delay event logs, notices within contractual time bars and approved variation valuation. Do not assume entitlement.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.10 Completion and turnover
Bridge-wise mechanical/structural completion records, punch lists, NDT/coating reports, material certificates, as-builts, removal/disposal records, service reinstatement and ONGC acceptance.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.
6.11 Financial and execution gate
CEO receives cost-to-complete, 13-week cash forecast, marine exposure, unresolved NCRs, critical-path risk and acceptance forecast; no unapproved spending or safety-gate waiver.
Required evidence / deliverable: controlled register or signed record, responsible manager, planned date, verification status, and escalation of any open technical, resource, commercial or operational constraint.

Formal review and release criteria
Before release, discipline leads must verify: (1) controlling contract and drawing revision; (2) reconciled quantity and scope; (3) qualified resources and documented capacity; (4) schedule and cost impact; (5) inspection and safety hold points; (6) ONGC and marine operational authorisations where applicable. CEO approval of a planning volume does not authorise offshore operations.
