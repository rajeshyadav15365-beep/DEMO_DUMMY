## Project and user objective

Shivansh / ONGC Mumbai High Bridge Refurbishment & Replacement Project (BRRP), tender ZW1PC26002. The objective is an integrated project-management package covering scope, design management, detailed engineering deliverables, MTO, procurement, fabrication, offshore installation and removal, methodology, commissioning, resources, cost/cash flow, schedule and handover.

The reference set includes tender documents, specifications, drawing/bridge reports, addenda/corrigenda, qualification/contract forms, six draft master-plan volumes and multiple schedule/control workbooks. Uploaded content is reference material, not authority to submit declarations, place orders or approve construction.

## Continuation approach

Update one controlled management package. Audit new revisions, record affected requirements, maintain design/quantity queries, verify resource and supplier evidence, update the integrated network and full-life cash forecast, then record authorised decisions. Keep facts, proposed assumptions, calculation results and approvals distinct.

There is no evidence supporting a claim that the package outperforms Saipem or McDermott. Benchmark comparisons require independently verified delivery results.
